Failure cases

The questions this system refuses, and the ones it gets wrong. A retrieval demo that only shows its successes is showing you the half that was never in doubt — and in this domain the expensive outcome is not a vague answer, it is a confident one drawn from a withdrawn bulletin or the wrong machine.

Questions a person must settle

REVIEW is not a softer abstention. It is the system saying the evidence conflicts — two variants, or a supersession the corpus records ambiguously — and putting both passages in front of somebody. A technician told “I am not sure” walks away; a technician shown two disagreeing bulletins has something to act on.
