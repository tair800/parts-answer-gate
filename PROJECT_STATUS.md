## Kill conditions

Reported in full in the README's **Where it falls short** section and in ADR-003. The short form:

- **E, F, I and K fail.** E and I are the gate over-covering in Turkish and Russian. F and K, like
  G's vacuity, trace to the effectivity filter sitting upstream of everything the criteria measure.
- **H passes at 10.3x**, and the pass is real — it is measured on the one class of question the
  filter cannot help with.
- **G passes near-vacuously**: for 297 of 312 hold-out questions its numerator is empty by
  construction. ADR-004 says so rather than banking the pass.
- No threshold was lowered, no kill test edited, and the gate was not adjusted after the hold-out
  was scored.

## Verified

- `make lint`, `make types` and the full suite pass locally against a real PostgreSQL 16 + pgvector
  and a local Qdrant.
- The bitemporal axis works end to end through the live HTTP API: the same question at the same
  `as_of` returns the correction under current knowledge and the original under the knowledge of
  the time.
- The split-leak guard catches three distinct planted leak shapes; it previously reduced to
  `split_of(F) != split_of(F)` and could not fail.
- The AST guard on the single answering site catches a second site planted in string form; it
  previously counted one spelling.
- Incremental re-embedding is measured through the loader rather than by re-hashing a hash.

## Known issues, published rather than tracked privately

1. **The gate over-covers in Turkish and Russian.** `gate.term_is_covered` approximates stemming
   with a bidirectional prefix match. This is the mechanism behind E's failure. Not adjusted,
   because the hold-out has been scored; a fix belongs to a new experiment with a new hold-out.
2. **G cannot fail as instrumented.** Disclosed in ADR-003.
3. **The managed-vector comparison is a local container.** Qdrant Cloud has never been reached and
   the artifact says so; the cost block publishes measured bytes and formulas with `null` where a
   price would go, because no vendor price list was read.
4. **No native-speaker evaluation and no LLM judge.** The multilingual result measures retrieval and
   gating over synthetic parallel text.
5. **No live model arm.** The abstractive answerer raises. No cost, latency or quality figure is
   published for any live arm.

## Deployment state

`render.yaml` is committed: Render free tier, Frankfurt, read-only, no model key, free PostgreSQL 16.
The Docker build regenerates the corpus from its committed seed and precomputes its vectors, so the
container loads an index rather than computing one — embedding this corpus takes about eleven
minutes on nine cores and would not finish on a shared-CPU instance before any health check gave up.
